# Career Intelligence Platform

> An AI-powered career intelligence platform that helps students understand career requirements, identify skill gaps, build relevant projects, improve their CVs, and track their employability progress.

---

##  Project Status

**Status: In Development**

The Career Intelligence Platform is currently in the data engineering and research phase.

The first career profile has been successfully extracted, cleaned, standardized, and processed using O*NET occupational data. The next stages will incorporate real job-market data, student skill profiles, AI-powered skill-gap analysis, project recommendations, and CV intelligence.

---

#  Problem

Many students choose a career based on a general idea of what the job involves without having a clear understanding of what employers actually expect.

For example, a student may say:

> "I want to become a Business Intelligence Analyst."

But they may not know:

- Which skills are actually required
- Which skills employers currently demand
- Which skills they already possess
- Which skills they are missing
- Which skills should be prioritized
- What projects would demonstrate those skills
- Whether their CV communicates their abilities effectively
- Whether their employability is improving over time

This creates a **skills visibility gap** between students and the labor market.

---

#  Proposed Solution

The Career Intelligence Platform aims to create a data-driven system that connects:

**Career requirements → Employer demand → Student skills → Skill gaps → Learning → Projects → CV → Employability progress**

The platform will use occupational data, job-market data, student evidence, and AI/NLP techniques to provide personalized career intelligence.

---

#  Core Objectives

The platform aims to help users:

1. Understand what a career actually requires.
2. Identify the skills employers are requesting.
3. Compare their current skills with career requirements.
4. Identify and prioritize skill gaps.
5. Receive relevant project recommendations.
6. Build evidence of their skills through projects.
7. Analyze how well their CV communicates their skills.
8. Track their employability development over time.

---

#  AI Component

AI will be an important part of the platform, but the system is **not yet fully implemented**.

The planned AI architecture will use structured career data as its foundation rather than allowing an AI model to independently invent career requirements.
